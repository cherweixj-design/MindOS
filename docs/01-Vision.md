# Vision

## Project Name

MindOS

## Tagline

Think Less. Build More.

## Mission

Help knowledge workers focus on creation, not repetition.

帮助知识工作者把时间留给创造，而不是重复劳动。

## Vision

打造 AI 时代值得信赖的个人 AI Workspace。

MindOS is an AI Workspace designed for product managers, developers, designers and AI learners.

Instead of becoming another AI Chat application, MindOS focuses on helping users manage documents, knowledge and daily work with AI.
